\subsection{{Artwork Restoration with Generative Image Inpainting | Python, PyTorch, git  \hfill September 2024 - December 2024}}
% \subtext{Magna New Mobility \hfill Toronto, ON, Canada}
\begin{zitemize}
    \item Designed a \textbf{controlled benchmarking study} to evaluate \textbf{3 generative inpainting models} (CO-MOD-GAN, ICT, LaMa) on the \textbf{WikiArt dataset}, training on \textbf{20k images} and testing on a \textbf{5k-image holdout set} with \textbf{5 degradation mask types}.

    \item Quantified restoration performance using \textbf{MSE, MS-SSIM, and FID}, revealing clear model trade-offs: LaMa achieved the best \textbf{pixel-level and structural fidelity} (MSE 0.01002, MS-SSIM 0.79205), while Co-Mod-GAN produced the most realistic global distributions (\textbf{FID 112.155}).

    \item Conducted \textbf{qualitative error analysis} across diverse art styles to identify \textbf{systematic failure modes}, including facial distortion, fine-detail degradation, and performance collapse under full occlusion.
\end{zitemize}
