\subsection{{Artwork Restoration with Generative Image Inpainting | Python, PyTorch, git  \hfill September 2024 - December 2024}}
% \subtext{Magna New Mobility \hfill Toronto, ON, Canada}
\begin{zitemize}
    \item Designed and executed a controlled benchmarking pipeline for \textbf{3 generative inpainting models} (CO-MOD-GAN, ICT, LaMa) on \textbf{WikiArt}, scaling training to \textbf{20k paintings} and evaluating on a \textbf{5k-image test set} with \textbf{5 degradation masks} to simulate real-world damage patterns.

    \item Analyzed model behavior using \textbf{MSE, MS-SSIM, and FID}, surfacing deployment-relevant trade-offs: LaMa delivered strongest \textbf{pixel and structural fidelity} (MSE 0.01002, MS-SSIM 0.79205), while Co-Mod-GAN better matched \textbf{global data distributions} (\textbf{FID 112.155}).

    \item Conducted systematic failure analysis across diverse art styles, identifying \textbf{robustness limits} such as facial distortion, fine-detail degradation, and breakdowns under full occlusion to inform model selection and usage constraints.
\end{zitemize}
